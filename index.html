Analog Electronics • PSpice & KiCad ~75% Voltage Transfer Efficiency

Multi-Stage Audio Amplifier & Low-Noise 2-Layer PCB

  • Simulated a multi-stage bipolar audio amplifier in PSpice featuring a 2N3904 differential input stage (Q1/Q2), a 2N3904/2N3906 voltage gain and driver stage (Q3/Q4) with 1N4148 diode biasing (D1), and a complementary TIP31/TIP32 emitter-follower output buffer (Q5/Q6).
  • Eliminated high source-resistance loading attenuation via the complementary emitter-follower stage, lowering effective output impedance to achieve ~75% voltage transfer efficiency across low-impedance loads.
  • Designed the 2-layer PCB in KiCad partitioned by functional blocks with a continuous ground plane, decoupling network (C5/C6), edge-mounted screw terminals (J1 Dual ±10V Supply, J2 Output, J3 Input), and isolated high-current output traces to suppress EMI.

Embedded Control • ArduKit & MATLAB -40% Water Usage

Closed-Loop Smart Irrigation Control System

  • Built an automated irrigation prototype on an ArduKit microcontroller integrating resistive soil moisture and ambient temperature sensors.
  • Implemented real-time hysteresis feedback control with thermal evaporation lockouts to trigger valve actuation dynamically rather than on fixed intervals.
  • Authored MATLAB telemetry scripts to benchmark multi-day sensor logs against fixed 12-hour schedules, verifying a 40% reduction in water consumption.

Numerical Computing • MATLAB 3D Spatial Pipeline

3D Structure from Motion & Spatial Reconstruction

  • Developed a computational pipeline in MATLAB to recover 3D point-cloud geometry from sequential 2D multi-view image matrices.
  • Implemented Gram-Schmidt orthonormalization, eigenvalue decomposition, and nonlinear root-finding (Newton-Raphson, Bisection, Secant) for radial lens distortion calibration.
  • Formulated Bicubic and Lanczos-3 interpolation kernels to evaluate sub-pixel upsampling fidelity and spatial reconstruction error bounds.

Electromechanical Drives • MATLAB RK4 & S-Curve Control

Electromechanical Drive System Simulation

  • Modeled electromagnetic torque production, stator/rotor equivalent circuit losses, and closed-loop speed regulation for PMSM and induction drive topologies in MATLAB.
  • Integrated coupled second-order differential equations using a 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) numerical solver under dynamic mechanical load steps.
  • Synthesized jerk-limited S-curve velocity profiles and damping optimization to suppress transient overshoot and mechanical torque ripple.
